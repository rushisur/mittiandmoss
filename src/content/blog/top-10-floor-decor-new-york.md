## Frequently Asked Questions About Floor Decor
### How do I choose the right size rug for a small NYC apartment?


For living rooms, I suggest ensuring at least the front legs of all major furniture pieces rest on the rug. In bedrooms, the rug should extend at least 18 inches beyond the sides of your bed to create balance.### What's the best way to incorporate greenery in a small space?


Tall, slender plants like snake plants or a [small olive tree](https://www.google.com/search?ved=1t:260882&q=small+olive+tree+in+pot&bbid=681091044507713918&bpid=1584706571657834177) in a floor planter work wonders. Consider hanging plants or wall-mounted options if floor space is extremely limited. Verticality is your friend!### Can floor decor really make a small space feel larger?


Absolutely! Using a large rug can visually expand a room by unifying furniture. Tall floor lamps and vases draw the eye upward, creating an illusion of height. Light colors and reflective surfaces also help immensely.### How do I clean floor decor items like poufs and rugs?


Always check the manufacturer's instructions first! For most rugs, regular vacuuming is key. Many poufs have removable, washable covers. For delicate items, gentle spot cleaning is usually the best approach.### What are the current floor decor trends?
