## Color & Materials That Set the Tone

<p data-end="1089" data-start="744">I anchor the palette with <strong data-end="812" data-start="770">warm whites, oat, greige, and mushroom** so the room glows at night and feels crisp by day. I add depth with <strong data-end="902" data-start="881">taupe microcement**, <strong data-end="916" data-start="904">[limewash](https://www.mittiandmoss.com/2025/10/limewash-living-rooms-warm-textured.html)**, and <strong data-end="936" data-start="922">aged brass** touches. I keep heavy contrast for accents like a bronze lamp or charcoal trim. Ever notice how a simple color tweak makes the whole room feel upgraded?

- 
<p data-end="1159" data-start="1120">Paint walls <strong data-end="1158" data-start="1132">warm white or mushroom**.

<p data-end="1208" data-start="1162">Add <strong data-end="1180" data-start="1166">wood tones**: light oak, walnut, or ash.

## Layout, Comfort, and Light

<p data-end="1635" data-start="1378">I plan seating for conversation first, TV second. I float rugs large enough to anchor every front leg, and I leave walkways clean. I layer lighting—ambient, task, accent—so nights feel soft and Instagram doesn’t need filters. FYI, dimmers change everything.
